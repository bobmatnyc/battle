Command language & multiplayer design PRODUCT INTENT



Voices of the period
Units and staff address you in language suited to the scenario’s era. In this demo: restrained Civil War dispatches, with clear meaning.
“General, we have reached the appointed position.”
Demo: period dispatch templates and agent instructions.


Localization by default
UI, dispatches, staff advice and scenario text are designed to support each player’s language, without changing the battle’s rules.
Requirement: i18n throughout. Current demo: English only.


Messages between players
An internal inbox will support direct and team dispatches. In-battle correspondence follows the scenario’s communication limits; staff chat is a separate channel.
Planned: player messaging. Current demo: human versus computer.




Different battles, different ways to play
Cooperative: share a mission with allied commanders. Antagonistic: lead opposing sides. Terrain control: contest and hold scenario-defined ground, individually or in teams.
Campaign fronts: a “Risk-like” strategic map for deploying armies and units across different fronts, assigning subordinate commanders and moving reserves. Local battles determine territory control and returning force strength; redeployment takes time.
Multiplayer and campaign scenarios are planned. This demo is one single-player summit defense.
