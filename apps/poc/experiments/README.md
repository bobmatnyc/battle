# Historical company-action experiments

The [six-era test matrix](six-era-cases.json) and [comparison plan](../../../docs/research/34-six-era-command-tests.md) define the current coverage. Keep this evaluator-only matrix away from controller prompts.

Implementation here follows the project's accepted-case rules. Each accepted case needs a stable ID, date/place, force and sector boundaries, cited orders or testimony with edition/page, historical outcome, contradictory accounts and unresolved inputs. Record documented facts separately from authored approximations (especially timings, terrain, strength and order translations). A company sector within a larger operation must identify the specific company, opposing element and time window; document the distinct capabilities and historical evidence for each era.

Use separate inputs for the two controllers' knowledge, scheduled intents and ex post historical outcome. Feed orders through normal authority and communications. Never give the historical outcome to controllers or use it to pick a winner. Freeze baseline rules before comparing cases. Where a historical action cannot be expressed, report it rather than silently substituting a different action.

For each run keep the scenario and rules versions, reproducible invocation, initial hash, accepted order trace/delivery times, mission outcome, force balances, simulation time and replay result. Compare ground/objective, timing, force condition and command behavior; distinguish agreement, disagreement and unmeasurable quantities. Run a sensitivity or counterfactual for a material assumption. A scripted-order mismatch cannot by itself establish a model-inference failure.

Evaluate the advanced-model run separately: commander takes the human side from authorized HQ observations, without future orders, truth or outcome labels. Verify the requested model and high effort actually supported/used, record legal and rejected proposals, latency, fallbacks, decision evidence and exact replay without fresh inference. Never label a fallback a model decision. Restrict public artifacts to reviewed, sanitized summaries, source links and fixtures.

The Little Round Top runner implements this path: `python3 -m battle --headless --player-side union --player-provider openrouter --model MODEL_ID --effort high`, with `--max-tokens` and `--timeout`. The opposing side is the explicit `scripted-fallback-v1` control. Each player decision records the requested and returned model, effort, latency, token usage, response id, raw proposed orders, validation result, rejected proposal and fallback flag with its stage and reason. The run metadata records the rules edition, scenario, prompt version, player side, provider, model, effort and opponent. Flags and field meanings: [POC README](../README.md#headless-player-runs). Live runs on 2026-11-06 confirmed the model ids `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5` and `openai/gpt-6-astra` and confirmed the `reasoning.effort` request shape: Astra reported 3,211 reasoning tokens for one decision, and a Sonnet probe reported 47. Those runs also showed Sonnet answering in prose despite `json_object`; the adapter now requests a strict `json_schema` and accepts exactly one order object from bare, fenced, prose-wrapped or tool-call replies. Fallbacks record the adapter's safe reason, such as `HTTP 429` or `TimeoutError`. Other cases need their own scenario catalogs before they can use this runner.

Copy the blank [scenario](template/scenario.json) and [evaluation](template/evaluation.json) templates per accepted case. Templates are scaffolding, not runs. The evaluation file stays outside the controller input; the runner delivers only each side’s current, authorized brief and due orders.

The additional [Cannae army-scale experiment](../../../docs/research/36-cannae-army-scale.md) uses separate formation and escape-lane criteria. Its [executable route checks](cannae/test_escape_routes.py) exercise the existing pathfinder.

Each case has two briefing-gallery pictures under its art directory. [Gallery index and download](../../../docs/research/battle-gallery/README.md). For Cannae, the art manifest and research file are self-contained in its art directory.
