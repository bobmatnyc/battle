# 52 — Pegasus Bridge: scenario plan and sources

**Date:** 2026-11-06 (UTC). **This is a plan, not a run.** It builds no entry, runs no simulation, changes no code and no fixture. It records what the sources say, what is inferred, and what the engine cannot yet do, so that a build can start from it. It follows [43](43-concord-scenario-spec.md); the selection came from [37](37-first-five-selection-register.md) (§4 row "Pegasus Bridge", §6 gaps) and the case from [34](34-six-era-command-tests.md).

Labels: **sourced** (S-id and locator), **inferred** (a reading from sourced facts; reasoning given), **reconstruction** (an authored scripted behaviour or value standing in for what no source states), **authored** (a POC choice with no source). Sources are in [09 — sources](09-sources.md#pegasus-bridge-source-pass--2026-11-06), S50 to S56, access date 2026-11-06.

## 1. Findings on the sources

**British order text: partly obtained.**
- S51 (Howard's coup-de-main report) and S52 (Toler's Operation Tonga report, Glider Pilot Regiment) were read in full as page images. Neither is the operation order. S51 is the company commander's report written in hospital, typed, with hand corrections; it states the task as briefed and the platoon tasks. S52 is a pilots' report with a one-paragraph statement of the task.
- Not obtained: the 6th Airborne Division or 6th Airlanding Brigade operation order, Howard's own order to D Company, any signal log, and any war diary (TNA WO 171 series; the 7th Parachute Battalion diary is WO 171/1239 per S54, paid copy).
- The brief's stop condition (no British order text at all) does not apply: the commander's report and the pilots' statement of the task exist. The orders themselves are reconstructed from them.
- Sheet 5 of the Tonga item on ParaData is an Exeter Ordnance Survey plan, not a Normandy map (S52). It is misfiled and is not used.

**What each primary source says** (locators are sheet or page and paragraph of the item):

| Point | S51 Howard report | S52 Tonga report |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | p. 1 para 2: "a coup de main task of capturing two vital bridges intact", the Caen Canal bridge at Bénouville and the Orne bridge at Ranville | Sheet 1 §2 "Task": land 6 gliders "in two fields adjacent to the bridges … secretly to achieve surprise and capture the bridges intact" |
| Hold until relieved | p. 1 para 3: "The defence of the Bridges, until our relief arrived, was expected to be a difficult task". Relief by 7th Parachute Battalion "reached us 3 hours after our landing, 2 hours later than expected" (so expected about one hour after landing). First relief was to be C Company, 7 Para, "dropped dispersed" | Not stated. Sheet 4 conclusion: both bridges taken "intact before either could be blown by the enemy" |
| Landing plan | p. 1 para 2: "Three Gliders were briefed to land within 50 yards of each bridge, speed and dash … was considered sufficient to overcome the German garrison of 50" | Sheet 1 §2: Nos 91 to 93 land in field X from south to north, Nos 94 to 96 in field Y from north to south; release 6,000 ft from the coast; tugs make diversionary bombing after release |
| Platoon tasks, canal bridge | p. 1 para 3: 25 Pln (Lt Brotheridge, with the company commander) "attacked and crossed the Br"; sappers cut wires and removed charges; 24 Pln (Lt Wood) "took on the inner defences"; 14 Pln (B Coy, Lt Smith) "ordered to reinforce 25 and start to form a small bridgehead to meet the first expected counter-attack" | Sheet 2: No. 92 carried 24 Pln (Wood) and Capt Neilson RE; No. 93 carried B Company (Lt Smith) |
| Platoon tasks, Orne bridge | p. 1 para 3: 17 Pln (B Coy, Lt Fox) and 23 Pln (Lt Sweeney); "Little opposition … 17 Pln soon had full control, reinforced by 23" | Sheet 3: No. 96 carried Lt Fox's platoon, which "took it with little opposition"; No. 95 (23 Pln, Sweeney) landed about 500 yards north, found the bridge "already taken" |
| Signals | None stated | None stated. Ham (canal bridge taken) and Jam (river bridge taken) come from S54 only |
| Outcome | p. 1 para 3: both bridges captured and consolidated "within 15 mins of landing"; p. 2 casualties 1 officer and 3 other ranks killed, 2 officers and 15 wounded, 3 missing | Sheet 4: 5 of 6 gliders landed near the objective; one landed completely away (released at the wrong point) |

**Conflicts between the sources read.**
- Landing time: S51 gives 00:25 for the first glider (p. 1 para 3); S53 says "just after midnight". Timing of the German tank approach: S51 says "within an hour" (about 01:25); S54 says 02:00. Relief by 7 Para: S51 says about three hours after landing; S54 says Pine-Coffin left the drop zone at 01:10; S53 says the Parachute Brigade arrived about 03:00. The plan uses S51 as the order-of-events spine and lists the others as variants.
- Platoon naming: S51 uses platoon numbers and names; S52 labels Nos 93 and 96 by "B Company" and the platoon commander. The two agree.
- S51's last paragraph says the regiment "landed and crossed the Bridge at 2300 hrs"; the date is not given and S54 puts the 2nd Royal Warwickshire at 21:15 that evening, so the line is ambiguous. Not used.

**German side: no primary record found.**
- Searched: the 716th Infantry Division and 736th Grenadier Regiment war diaries, a Bundesarchiv holding, Schmidt's own account, Luck's memoir and a German-language Pegasus history. None was available online or retrievable in this pass.
- What exists is secondary. S54 says the bridges were guarded by 50 men of the 736th Grenadier Regiment under Major Hans Schmidt; that "Schmidt's soldiers had orders to blow up the two bridges if they were in danger of capture"; that two sentries were on duty; and gives a response timeline (01:20 Richter orders 21st Panzer to attack; 01:30 Luck's regiment moves; 02:00 attack from the west). S54 cites no primary for those, and cites a single German-language item (Keusgen), for a denial that machine guns stood by the canal bridge. S55 says Feuchtinger refused Luck permission to attack, citing orders, with Mitcham (1983) as its source.
- S51 (p. 1 para 2) gives the British planning figure of "50" Germans. That is a British estimate of the garrison, not a German record, and it agrees with S54.
- **German intent is therefore inferred**, not sourced. Reasoning: the garrison's orders to blow the bridges are secondary; the sentries' response (one fled, one fired a flare, S54) and the flare seen from glider No. 93 before landing (S52 sheet 2) are the only direct evidence of the alarm; the divisional response is documented only as a delay. No German order to the guard, and no German account of what its officer intended after the landing, was read.

## 2. Scenario frame (proposal)

- **Action.** The canal-bridge platoons of D Company and attached B Company platoons, 2nd Ox and Bucks, in the two-bridge operation, 6 June 1944 (fixture case `benouville`, [six-era-cases.json](../../apps/poc/experiments/six-era-cases.json)). Player side: British (the fixture asks whether the platoons seize the crossing intact before the defence reacts). Computer side: German.
- **Start.** Touchdown at 00:25 (S51 p. 1 para 3), the order of events starting at the first glider. The fixture's own gap line says "begin at touchdown". Start time: sourced (S51); the minute is the report's own.
- **Scale.** 50 m a hex, 30 seconds a turn (engine constants, [43 §1](43-concord-scenario-spec.md)). Authored.
- **Scope decision (open, §7).** Recommended: the canal bridge only, with the Orne bridge as a second objective in a variant, because the fixture and [34](34-six-era-command-tests.md) follow the canal-bridge platoons and the Orne bridge is "a separate objective". The two bridges stand about 500 m apart (S54), ten hexes; a 24 x 21 grid (1,200 m by 1,050 m) holds both.

## 5. Objectives and candidate victory rule

- **Objectives.** British: the canal bridge, intact and passable, held until relief. German: stop that, or destroy the bridge. Source of the historical outcome: S51, S52 sheet 4, S50.
- **Candidate rule: seize, then hold.** The British win when a British detachment is on the bridge hex and its radius-1 neighbours with no effective German in them for `hold_turns`, and the bridge is not destroyed, and they remain there until a scenario-declared relief event or the deadline. The German win by holding the bridge or destroying it. This is the existing `hold-objective` kind ([43 §3 row 7](43-concord-scenario-spec.md)) plus two things the engine lacks: a bridge state (demolition) and a relief event (see §8). Authored values.
- **Variants of the rule** (open, §7): seize only (no hold clause); the deadline at 7 Para's arrival; both bridges.

## 6. Units and scale

- **British.** Six gliders: platoons 25, 24 and 14 for the canal bridge; 17 and 23 for the Orne bridge; 22 missing (landed near the River Dives; S51 p. 2; S52 sheet 2 No. 94). Platoon strengths are not in S51 or S52; the number is authored. Sappers (Capt Neilson, 249 Field Company) were attached (S52 sheet 2; S54). Total British casualties in S51 p. 2.
- **German.** About 50 men of the 736th Grenadier Regiment, of mixed nationalities (S54), as detachments of authored size summing to about 50. S51 gives the same planning figure. The reserve (armour, infantry, guns) arrives by schedule (§8).
- **Echelon and scale.** Platoon detachments; `poc-10` default detachment is 40, which is more than a platoon; the entry would set a smaller authored value and note it.
- **Not used.** The Orne-bridge platoons for the canal-bridge variant; the 7 Para and commando units appear only as a relief event.

## 10. Open questions and candidate variants

1. **Scope:** the canal bridge alone, or both bridges? Candidate variants: A (canal only), B (canal plus the Orne bridge as a second objective), C (the Orne bridge's platoons alone).
2. **German intent:** the demolition order is secondary (S54) and the response is delayed by orders (S55). Candidate variants: guard-only (no demolition), demolition on contact, demolition on first crossing.
3. **Start:** at touchdown (the fixture), or including the approach with the flare seen at No. 93. Candidate: start at 00:20 with the platoons not yet landed, needing arrival scheduling (gap 2).
4. **Relief timing:** S51 says three hours after landing; S54 gives earlier events. Candidate: relief at 01:25 (expected), 03:25 (actual), or not modelled.
5. **Platoon strengths and the German split:** not sourced. Authored.
6. **Which bank holds the café and the pillbox.** Not stated in the sources read.
7. **The 22 Platoon and the Orne bridge's reinforcements:** left out of scope.
8. **Sources not read:** the Division order, a war diary, German records, IWM page, Luck, Ambrose, Keusgen. Which pulls are wanted before any run?

**Top three for the owner:** the scope (1), the German intent variant (2), the relief timing (4).
